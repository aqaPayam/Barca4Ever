# Multi-agent integration selection

The independent neural, directed-boundary, physics-boundary and predeclared
composite experiments each select parameters on WR9 before their matched
whole-match CV. Only qualifying choices confirm on reused WMX/WN1 regression.
Symmetry averaging failed CV and is not eligible. Failed individual candidates
are not reselected from their regression outcomes.

Among complete candidates passing their own declared checks, select the highest
WR9 mean spell F1, macro F1 breaking ties. Validation ranks candidates; the
regression scores are eligibility evidence, not a numerical ranking selector.
The anchored missing-input adapter is a separately declared Pareto experiment
that may preserve clean predictions exactly and must improve noisy performance;
this does not relax any previous failed experiment's acceptance criteria.

All seven external games have appeared in development. Architecture acceptance
uses these repeated comparisons; it is not fresh blind evaluation or evidence
of professional-system superiority. No competition input, video, labels or
prediction quality enters any fitting, selection or score. V4.1 stays active
until the winner passes saved-model CPU and synthetic checks.
